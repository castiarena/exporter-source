Quarterly Garden Report

Twelve raised beds, one very dry July, and what we would plant again.

The summer quarter closed with the best tomato yield the plot has produced, despite three weeks without meaningful rain. Mulching early and moving the drip lines under the straw did most of the work; the rest came down to picking varieties that shrug off heat rather than fighting it.
